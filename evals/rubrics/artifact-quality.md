# Artifact Quality Rubric

**Purpose:** Evaluate final artifact quality across completeness, correctness, clarity, and constraint adherence.

---

## Scoring Dimensions

### 1. Completeness (0-10 points)

**Definition:** All requirements from brief are addressed in final artifact.

**Scoring:**
- **10 points:** All requirements fully addressed, no gaps
- **8 points:** All requirements addressed, minor gaps in depth
- **6 points:** Most requirements addressed, some gaps
- **4 points:** Many requirements addressed, significant gaps
- **2 points:** Few requirements addressed, major gaps
- **0 points:** Requirements largely unaddressed

**Evaluation criteria:**
- ✅ All in_scope items from brief present in artifact
- ✅ All success criteria from brief can be evaluated
- ✅ No placeholder sections (e.g., [To be written])
- ✅ Required depth achieved (not just surface coverage)

**Common failures:**
- ❌ Section mentioned in outline but missing from draft
- ❌ Success criterion from brief not addressable in artifact
- ❌ Placeholder content in final artifact
- ❌ Surface-level coverage where depth required

---

### 2. Correctness (0-10 points)

**Definition:** Artifact contains no factual errors, logical inconsistencies, or contradictions.

**Scoring:**
- **10 points:** No errors, fully consistent
- **8 points:** 1-2 minor errors, no logical issues
- **6 points:** 3-5 minor errors or 1 logical inconsistency
- **4 points:** Multiple errors or logical issues
- **2 points:** Significant errors or contradictions
- **0 points:** Pervasive errors, unusable

**Evaluation criteria:**
- ✅ Factual claims supported by sources
- ✅ No internal contradictions
- ✅ Logical flow (conclusions follow from premises)
- ✅ Technical accuracy (if applicable)

**Common failures:**
- ❌ Unsupported factual claims
- ❌ Section 2 contradicts Section 5
- ❌ Conclusion doesn't follow from evidence
- ❌ Technical details incorrect

---

### 3. Clarity (0-10 points)

**Definition:** Artifact is readable, well-structured, and appropriate for target audience.

**Scoring:**
- **10 points:** Exceptionally clear, perfect structure
- **8 points:** Very clear, good structure
- **6 points:** Mostly clear, adequate structure
- **4 points:** Somewhat unclear, weak structure
- **2 points:** Confusing, poor structure
- **0 points:** Incomprehensible

**Evaluation criteria:**
- ✅ Vocabulary appropriate for audience
- ✅ Sentence structure clear (no run-ons, fragments)
- ✅ Heading hierarchy logical (H1 → H2 → H3, no skips)
- ✅ Transitions between sections smooth
- ✅ Examples clarify concepts (if applicable)

**Common failures:**
- ❌ Jargon used without explanation for non-expert audience
- ❌ Run-on sentences, unclear phrasing
- ❌ Heading hierarchy skips levels (H1 → H3)
- ❌ Abrupt section transitions
- ❌ Concepts explained without examples where needed

---

### 4. Constraint Adherence (0-10 points)

**Definition:** Artifact meets all specified constraints (format, length, style, structure).

**Scoring:**
- **10 points:** All constraints met perfectly
- **8 points:** All constraints met, minor deviations
- **6 points:** Most constraints met, some deviations
- **4 points:** Many constraints violated
- **2 points:** Significant constraint violations
- **0 points:** Constraints largely ignored

**Evaluation criteria:**
- ✅ Word count within specified range (±10%)
- ✅ Format requirements met (markdown, headings, etc.)
- ✅ Style pack applied (if specified)
- ✅ Template structure followed (if specified)
- ✅ Hard constraints respected (e.g., no profanity, PG-13)

**Common failures:**
- ❌ Word count 50% over/under target
- ❌ Required format not used (e.g., JSON instead of markdown)
- ❌ Style pack ignored (e.g., formal style when casual required)
- ❌ Template structure not followed
- ❌ Hard constraint violated (e.g., profanity in PG-13 content)

---

## Total Score

**Maximum:** 40 points

**Interpretation:**
- **36-40:** Excellent artifact, production-ready
- **30-35:** Good artifact, minor revisions needed
- **24-29:** Acceptable artifact, moderate revisions needed
- **18-23:** Weak artifact, significant revisions needed
- **12-17:** Poor artifact, major rework required
- **0-11:** Unusable artifact, start over
